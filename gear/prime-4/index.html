    Connections and controls

    Zone out for a second room
    Zone Out mirrors the master until Zone Channel Assign puts deck 4 on it as a zone playlist. Then channel 4's level and EQ are bypassed and the Zone knobs take over.
    Useful for a lobby feed off the same console; not simulated here.
