    Connections and controls
    Each button only reads back what the manufacturer document says that connector or control does.
    

    Sub and top together
    The ZLX QuickSmartDSP has a sub/top system-match preset. With a sub below it, set the top to the matching preset so the crossover is shared rather than doubled, and feed the sub from the MIX OUT or split the signal before the top.
    Preset names and the exact crossover point are in the EDS and manual; this model does not simulate DSP.

    Electro-Voice's official English EDS gives the envelope (685 × 426 × 383 mm), net weight (17.3 kg) and transducers (EVS-15L 15-inch woofer, DH-1K HF driver), along with the amplifier, SPL, response and coverage figures. Retailer copies of the EV table agree with it.
