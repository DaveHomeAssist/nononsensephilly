    Connections and controls
    Each button only reads back what the manufacturer document says that connector or control does.
    

    Powering the S-181 subs
    Each modified S-181 is 8 Ω nominal. One per channel in FULL RANGE with an external crossover, or two in parallel per channel at 4 Ω. In CROSSOVER mode channel 1 is the sub feed and channel 2 the top, from one input.
    Check the amplifier label first: GX7 gives 725 W at 8 Ω, GX5 500 W.
