An FAQ story

Carry over one small campaign motif. Build a dark, low-detail text area for a question and a short answer. Give dates, hours, and location their own slides when that clarifies the message.

AfterJinx FAQ story copy

Format notes

  • 4:5 feed keeps the title, focal image, and first billing tier visible at thumbnail size. Use a clearly defined footer.
  • 9:16 story gives the layout more height. Reflow the billing or split a dense announcement across slides. Keep key text clear of top and bottom interface overlays.
  • 4 × 6 in print is a 2:3 trim ratio. Confirm the printer's bleed and safe area, extend edge artwork through the bleed, and keep type and any real QR code inside the safe area.
  • FAQ story uses editable type over a simple plate. Keep one answer per slide and verify midnight date wording against current event copy.
