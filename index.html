      The collective

      About No Nonsense Collective

      No Nonsense is a Philadelphia event and production collective. We produce after-hours events, book talent, and support rooms with production rentals and crew. The standard is simple: clear expectations, clean execution, and no surprises.

      • Clear entryTicket, age, ID, and re-entry rules are published ahead of time and enforced consistently.
      • Transparent pricingAny ticket fees are shown before checkout. Rental pricing is scoped and confirmed in writing.
      • Built for the roomWe plan the production around the space, the audience, and what the night actually needs.
      • Direct bookingArtists, promoters, and partners get a clear answer, a defined scope, and a practical next step.

      Clear expectations before doors. Clean execution once they open.

      Before the night

      FAQ

      Unless an event lists a different minimum age, entry is 18+ and alcohol service is 21+. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID. If an event requires a name match, the ticket page will say so.

      If we cancel, the ticket face value is refunded to the original payment method. Missed events are not refundable. Transfers are only available when the event allows them.

      Public venues are listed on the event page. For private or quiet rooms, the exact address is released at 6 PM on the day of the event through the ticket and text notification.

      Use the contact form and choose Booking & Talent. Include the date window, venue or room, event format, expected draw, and relevant artist links. We’ll respond with availability or a better-fit date.

      Each ticket admits one person once per session unless re-entry is explicitly recorded. Duplicate or copied codes are rejected.

      Ask door staff before leaving. Re-entry only works when the event allows it and your ticket is marked before you exit.

      Some events are no-photo. The event page will say so before you buy. Please respect the room and the people on the floor. Free earplugs are available at the merch table.

      Contact us before the event with what you need. We’ll tell you what the room can support and flag any limitations early.
