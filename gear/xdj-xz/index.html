    Simulated XDJ-XZ state

    

    Connections and controls
    Each button only reads back what the manufacturer document says that connector or control does.
    

    Four decks from one box
    Decks 1 and 2 are the jogs. Decks 3 and 4 come from the LINE/PHONO inputs, a Pro DJ Link player on the LINK port, or a laptop on USB running rekordbox or Serato.
    The XZ can take the CDJ-3000s on channels 3 and 4 if the A9 is out.
