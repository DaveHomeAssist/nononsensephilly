    Connections and controls
    Each button only reads back what the manufacturer document says that connector or control does.
    

    Mic into the A9
    The SM58 goes into MIC 1 or MIC 2 on XLR. Leave phantom power off for a dynamic mic; it is only there for condensers on MIC 1.
    Talkover and feedback reducer are top-panel functions, not simulated here.
